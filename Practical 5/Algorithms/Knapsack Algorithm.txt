Algorithm: 0/1 Knapsack (Dynamic Programming)

Aim:
To find the maximum profit that can be obtained by selecting items
without exceeding the knapsack capacity. Each item can be taken at
most once (0/1).

Input:
- Number of items n
- Weight of each item
- Profit (value) of each item
- Knapsack capacity W

Output:
- Maximum profit
- Execution time and complexity

Steps:
1. Start
2. Read n, weights[], values[], and capacity W
3. Create a DP table dp[n+1][W+1] and initialize all cells to 0
4. For i = 1 to n, repeat steps 5 to 8
5.    For w = 1 to W, repeat steps 6 to 8
6.       If weights[i-1] <= w then
            take = values[i-1] + dp[i-1][w - weights[i-1]]
            skip = dp[i-1][w]
            dp[i][w] = maximum of (take, skip)
7.       Else
            dp[i][w] = dp[i-1][w]   (item too heavy, skip)
8. Continue
9. Maximum profit = dp[n][W]
10. Print maximum profit
11. Stop

Idea:
For every item and every capacity, decide:
- Take the item (if it fits), OR
- Skip the item
Choose the option that gives more profit.

Example:
Weights = [2, 3, 4, 5]
Profits = [3, 4, 5, 6]
Capacity = 5
Maximum profit = 7  (items with weight 2 and 3)

Time Complexity: O(n * W)
Space Complexity: O(n * W)
